Simple 4-Step Process

How Eco-Tracker Works

A streamlined, transparent workflow connecting citizens, admins, and certified collectors.

1. Request Pickup

Choose E-Waste or Dry-Waste, specify estimated quantity, enter your address, and select your preferred time slot.

2. Admin Review

The admin reviews item suitability, approves the schedule, and assigns a verified local collector.

3. Doorstep Pickup

The collector arrives within the confirmed window, checks materials, safely loads items, and logs collection outcome.

4. Earn Rewards

Recycling facility confirms weight. You receive reward points in your ledger and build up your streak badge!

Responsible Segregation

What We Collect

Proper classification ensures safe treatment and prevents toxic landfill contamination.

E-Waste (Electronic Waste)

High Impact Category

Safe dismantling and precious metal extraction for all electronic and battery-operated equipment.

Accepted Items:
  • Old laptops, computers, CRT/LED monitors, cables & chargers
  • Defunct smartphones, tablets, handheld electronics
  • Household appliances: microwaves, toasters, blenders
  • Lithium-ion batteries and electronic circuit boards
Reward: 50 base pts + 10 pts per kg

Dry Recyclable Waste

Clean Segregated Materials

Clean, unsoiled solid household and office packaging materials sent directly to licensed recycling mills.

Accepted Items:
  • Corrugated cardboard boxes, magazines, office paper
  • PET plastic bottles, containers, clean poly bags
  • Metal tins, aluminum soda cans, scrap copper/iron
  • Glass bottles and unbroken glass packaging
Reward: 20 base pts + 5 pts per kg
